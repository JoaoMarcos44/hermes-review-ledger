# Ledger visual identity

Ledger is a fan tribute to Heath Ledger and his portrayal of the Joker; no official affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

- `ledger-pixel.gif`: silent 10-second looping animation, 640 × 360, 12 fps, 48-color palette.
- `ledger-pixel.png`: static first-frame alternative, 640 × 360.

The user-supplied animated reference was transformed to a 160 × 90 pixel grid, enlarged 4× with nearest-neighbor scaling, and quantized to a limited palette. This is a pixel-styled conversion of the original motion, not a claim of hand-drawn animation. No audio track is included. The original video and its audio are not part of this repository change.

These files do not grant rights to underlying third-party characters or reference artwork.
